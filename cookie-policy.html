Cookie Policy

A brief overview of how cookies work on our website and how you control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit our website. They help us remember your navigation choices, secure form submissions, and maintain site functionality.

2. Cookies We Use

We believe in minimal, purposeful cookie usage:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site operation, navigation, and submitting quote or sample requests. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your banner consent and viewing preferences across visits.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, non-identifying metrics that help us understand page popularity and optimize website performance.

Note: We do not use third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or behavioral tracking across external sites.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can easily control, block, or delete cookies at any time through your web browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge). Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to submit quote or sample request forms.
