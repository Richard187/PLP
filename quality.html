Our Quality Philosophy

Quality is Controlled, Not Just Checked

At Prime Leaf Processing, quality is not a box ticked at the end of a production run. It is a continuous process of control, verification and correction applied at every stage of the processing cycle — from the moment tobacco enters our facility to the moment it leaves.

Our multi-stage quality system is built around a simple principle: it is better to catch a deviation early than to discover it in a finished product.

Every production batch is produced to an agreed customer specification. Our quality system is designed to verify compliance at each stage and to ensure that finished product meets that specification before it is released for dispatch.

Quality System

Nine Quality Checkpoints

Every batch passes through nine defined quality checkpoints before dispatch.

01

Incoming Leaf Inspection

All incoming tobacco leaf inspected against agreed grade and quality specifications before entering the processing cycle. Non-compliant material is identified and segregated at this stage.

02

Moisture Control

Moisture levels monitored at multiple points — during conditioning, post-cutting, during drying and before packaging. Final moisture is verified against the agreed specification.

03

Grade Verification

Tobacco grade verified against customer order at key processing stages. Ensures that the product being processed meets the agreed grade specification throughout the run.

04

Cut-Width Consistency

Cut-width measurements taken at regular intervals during cutting operations. Any deviation is identified and corrected promptly to maintain specification compliance across the batch.

05

Foreign Material Control

Non-tobacco material and foreign material controls applied throughout processing. Multiple control points used to identify and remove foreign material from the production stream.

06

Blend Verification

Where blended products are being processed, blend composition is verified against the agreed formulation before and during blending operations.

07

Physical Inspection

Physical characteristics of processed tobacco assessed during and after processing to ensure product integrity and consistency with agreed specifications.

08

Packaging Inspection

Finished product packaging inspected before dispatch. Condition, weight, labelling and packaging integrity verified for each batch before release.

09

Final Product Release

Final product only released for shipment after all quality checks are satisfied and the batch record is complete. Product is held if any checkpoint is not cleared.

Batch Traceability

Full Batch Traceability

Raw Material Records:Source, type, grade and condition of incoming tobacco leaf for each batch.
Processing Records:Processing parameters, quality check results and any deviations identified during production.
Quality Records:Results of all quality checks conducted during and after processing, referenced against specification.
Dispatch Records:Packaging, labelling and dispatch documentation for each batch released for shipment.
