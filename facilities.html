Our Location

BEPZA EZ, Chattogram

Prime Leaf Processing operates from Plot # 528A in the BEPZA Export Processing Zone in Chattogram, Bangladesh — one of the country's key export-oriented industrial zones.

Chattogram is Bangladesh's principal port city and commercial hub, providing direct access to international shipping routes and container terminals. Operating within the BEPZA EPZ provides regulatory support for export-oriented manufacturing and processing businesses.

Facility Capabilities

What Our Facility Provides

Our Chattogram facility is equipped for the full tobacco leaf processing and cut-rag manufacturing cycle.

01

Leaf Processing Area

Dedicated area for raw tobacco leaf receiving, inspection, grading and initial preparation before processing.

02

Conditioning Plant

Controlled tobacco conditioning equipment for pre-processing moisture and physical conditioning.

03

Cutting Lines

Precision tobacco cutting equipment capable of producing cut-rag to agreed customer cut-width and cut-length specifications.

04

Drying Facility

Controlled drying operations for moisture management of cut-rag tobacco following cutting operations.

05

Blending Area

Blending operations area for production of customer-specific tobacco blends to agreed formulations.

06

Quality Control Lab

Quality control area for moisture testing, physical inspection and batch verification against customer specifications.

07

Packaging Area

Packaging facility for bulk, carton and case packaging of finished processed tobacco products for export.

08

Warehouse & Storage

Controlled storage for raw tobacco, work-in-progress and finished products awaiting dispatch.

09

Export Logistics

Direct connection to Chattogram port for international container shipment. Full export documentation support.
