About Prime Leaf Processing

Bangladesh-based tobacco leaf processing and cut-rag manufacturing for international B2B customers.

Who We Are

A Specialist Tobacco Processing Partner

Prime Leaf Processing is a Bangladesh-based tobacco processing company specialising in cut-rag and tobacco leaf processing for professional B2B customers. Operating from our facility at BEPZA EZ in Chattogram, we provide specification-driven processing solutions for tobacco manufacturers, traders and international buyers.

Our focus is on industrial tobacco processing — the transformation of raw tobacco leaf into consistent, finished processed tobacco and cut-rag products that meet agreed customer specifications, batch after batch.

We are not a cigarette brand, a consumer tobacco company, or a retail tobacco business. We are an industrial processing partner for serious B2B customers in the international tobacco industry.
