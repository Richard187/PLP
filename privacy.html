Privacy Policy

How Prime Leaf Processing collects, uses, and safeguards your business information.

1. Overview & Who We Are

Prime Leaf Processing ("PLP", "we", "our") operates from Plot # 528A, BEPZA Export Processing Zone, Chattogram, Bangladesh. We provide B2B tobacco leaf processing and cut-rag manufacturing exclusively to professional commercial partners.

We respect your privacy. This policy clearly outlines what business details we collect, why we need them, and how we keep them safe.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to fulfill your business inquiries and orders:

  • Commercial Contact Details: Name, job title, company name, business email, telephone number, and delivery destination when submitting forms or requesting quotes.
  • Inquiry & Blend Specifications: Leaf grades, cut-rag requirements, blend parameters, and delivery timelines you provide.
  • Technical Site Data: Anonymous browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected strictly to maintain website security and performance.

We do not collect sensitive personal data or consumer payment card information on this website.

3. How We Use Information

Your details are used strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:

  • Providing quotations, technical specifications, and processing leaf samples.
  • Managing communication, manufacturing schedules, and international logistics.
  • Complying with customs, export regulations, and corporate tax laws.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third parties.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We only share data with vetted service partners directly involved in fulfilling your commercial requests:

  • Logistics & Customs: Authorized international shipping lines and freight handlers to deliver samples or shipments.
  • Secure Infrastructure: Trusted enterprise IT and hosting services bound by strict confidentiality.

All data is protected using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted access controls.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You may request a copy of your records, request updates to your details, or ask us to delete your commercial contact records at any time.
