About Prime Leaf Processing

Built Around Quality, Consistency and Precision

Prime Leaf Processing is a Bangladesh-based tobacco processing company focused on transforming carefully selected tobacco leaf into consistent, specification-driven processed tobacco and cut-rag products for professional B2B customers.

Operating from our facility in the BEPZA Export Processing Zone in Chattogram, we work with tobacco manufacturers, international trading companies, importers and procurement teams seeking a reliable industrial processing partner.

Processing Expertise

End-to-end tobacco processing from leaf conditioning through to finished cut-rag, managed by experienced processing engineers.

Consistent Specifications

Batch-to-batch consistency is central to our operations. Customer moisture, cut-width, and filling power specs are strictly maintained.

International B2B Focus

Duty-free BEPZA EPZ location offering direct containerized shipments via Chattogram seaport with comprehensive export documentation.

Industrial Cut-Rag & Stem Expansion Solutions

Cut-Rag Tobacco

Precision-cut filler manufactured in 0.60mm to 0.90mm cut widths. American blends, Virginia Flue-Cured, English, RYO, and MYO profiles with superior strand integrity.

  • ±0.5% in-line moisture control

Cut Rolled Expanded Stems (CRES)

High-expansion stem line delivering up to +35% filling power boost. Significantly optimizes cigarette rod firmness, burn uniformity, and cost-per-thousand sticks.

  • +35% filling power enhancement
  • Clean thermal steam expansion
  • Ideal for 15% – 30% blend inclusion
