Our Sourcing Approach

Where Quality Begins

Quality processing starts with quality raw material. Prime Leaf Processing is committed to sourcing raw tobacco leaf from supply networks that share our commitment to quality, consistency and responsible agricultural practice.

Our sourcing decisions are guided by customer requirements, quality standards and commercial best practice. We work to ensure that the tobacco entering our processing facility meets agreed standards from the point of origin — not just when it arrives at our door.

We maintain ongoing relationships with our leaf suppliers and conduct regular assessment of supply chain quality and compliance. Where customers have specific origin or sourcing requirements, we work to accommodate these within our supply network.

Our Sourcing Priorities

Quality and grade specification compliance from raw material

Supply consistency and reliability

Origin traceability from leaf to finished product

Long-term supplier relationship development

Supply Chain Standards

What We Require from Our Supply Chain

01

Quality Standards

Suppliers are required to provide tobacco leaf that meets agreed grade and quality specifications. Non-compliant material is identified and rejected at intake inspection.

02

Documentation

Full documentation for all incoming leaf including origin, grade, weight and any applicable certifications or declarations required by customers or regulations.

03

Traceability

Origin information is recorded for all incoming leaf to support batch traceability from source through to finished product dispatch.

04

Responsible Practices

We seek to work with suppliers who demonstrate responsible agricultural and business practices and who share our commitment to operating responsibly.

05

Supply Reliability

Our supply relationships are built for reliability — consistent quality, consistent availability and consistent performance over time.

06

Customer-Specific Sourcing

Where customers specify origin requirements, blend ratios or other sourcing parameters, we work within our supply network to fulfil these requirements.
