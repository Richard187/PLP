Responsible Business Statement

Prime Leaf Processing's approach to responsible B2B operations.

Our B2B Status

Prime Leaf Processing is a business-to-business (B2B) industrial tobacco processing company. We process tobacco leaf and supply processed tobacco products exclusively to professional commercial customers in the tobacco industry — manufacturers, traders, importers and sourcing organisations. We do not sell to or market to consumers.

Regulatory Compliance

We comply with applicable regulations in Bangladesh governing tobacco processing, export and business operations. We respect the right of each jurisdiction to regulate tobacco products and their trade in accordance with their own legal and public health frameworks. Customers are responsible for compliance with all applicable regulations in their own markets.

No Health Claims

Prime Leaf Processing does not make any health claims or health benefit statements with respect to tobacco or processed tobacco products. We acknowledge the established scientific consensus on the health risks associated with tobacco use and do not dispute or misrepresent this evidence.

Marketing Conduct

Our marketing and commercial communications are directed exclusively at professional B2B audiences in the tobacco industry. We do not market to, or seek to market to, individuals under the legal age for tobacco products in any jurisdiction. This website is intended for the information of professional commercial buyers and industry professionals only.

Supply Chain Responsibility

We are committed to responsible conduct throughout our supply chain. We work with suppliers who operate to appropriate standards and seek to maintain supply chain traceability. We do not knowingly support or facilitate illegal or irresponsible practices in our supply network.

Employment & Labour Standards

We are committed to fair employment practices and to providing safe, respectful working conditions for all our employees. We do not condone forced labour, child labour or any form of employment practice that is inconsistent with applicable law and fundamental labour rights.

Environmental Awareness & Resource Efficiency

Prime Leaf Processing operates with proactive awareness of our environmental footprint across all factory operations. We continuously monitor and seek to improve energy efficiency across our rotary drying and steam conditioning circuits. We are committed to minimising processing waste and identifying responsible utilisation or disposal routes for tobacco processing by-products.

Community Engagement

Operating within the BEPZA Economic Zone in Chattogram, we maintain positive relationships with local stakeholders, municipal bodies, and the industrial community, contributing positively through quality industrial employment and ethical business conduct.

Transparency & Reporting

We are committed to being open and transparent about our operations, quality standards, and compliance performance. We provide qualified commercial customers with specification certificates, laboratory analysis reports, and compliance documentation upon request.

Contact

If you have questions about our responsible business practices, please contact us at info@primeleafp.com.

Last updated: 2025. Prime Leaf Processing reserves the right to update this statement as our operations and commitments develop.
