Sustainability

Our commitment to responsible processing across our supply chain, operations and business relationships.

Our Position

Responsible Operations

Prime Leaf Processing is committed to operating responsibly across every aspect of our business — from the supply chain and processing operations to our workforce and the communities in which we operate.

As a B2B industrial tobacco processing company, we recognise that our customers — and increasingly their customers — expect responsible business conduct and transparency in supply chain operations. Our sustainability commitments are designed to meet these expectations with genuine action.

Our Commitments

Seven Areas of Commitment

Responsible Sourcing

Working with leaf suppliers who operate responsibly and to agreed standards. Seeking traceability from raw material source through to finished product.

Energy Awareness

Working towards greater awareness and management of energy use across our processing operations. Identifying opportunities to improve energy efficiency.

Waste Reduction

Committed to minimising processing waste and identifying responsible disposal and utilisation routes for tobacco processing by-products and operational waste.

Workforce & Fair Employment

Committed to fair employment practices, safe working conditions and respect for the rights and dignity of all our employees.

Community Engagement

Maintaining positive relationships with local communities in Chattogram and contributing positively through employment and responsible business conduct.

Environmental Awareness

Operating with awareness of our environmental footprint and working to reduce our impact through improved operational practices.

Transparency & Reporting

Committed to being open about our sustainability performance, providing customers with information about our supply chain and operations when required.

Responsible Business Notice

B2B Industrial Operations Only

Prime Leaf Processing is an industrial tobacco processing company operating exclusively in the B2B market. We do not sell tobacco products to consumers. We do not make health claims or health benefit statements. We comply with applicable regulations in Bangladesh governing tobacco processing and export. We support the right of each jurisdiction to regulate tobacco products in accordance with their own legal and public health frameworks.
