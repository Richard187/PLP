Logistics & Export

From our Chattogram facility to your destination — reliable export logistics for processed tobacco.

Export Process

From Factory to Customer

Our export logistics process is designed to ensure your processed tobacco arrives in the agreed condition, on time, with full documentation.

01

Production Completion

All production and quality control completed and verified against the agreed specification before packaging and dispatch preparation begins.

02

Packaging

Product packaged to agreed specification — bulk, carton, or case — and inspected before sealing. Labels and batch identification applied.

03

Warehouse & Staging

Finished packaged product held in warehouse under appropriate conditions pending shipment booking and documentation preparation.

04

Documentation Preparation

Preparation of all required export documentation — commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin and any destination-specific documentation.

05

Container Loading

Product loaded into container at our facility or at Chattogram port. Loading supervised and container sealed for international shipment.

06

Port & Customs Clearance

Export customs clearance and port handling at Chattogram port. Processing within the BEPZA EPZ provides export facilitation support.

07

International Shipping

Ocean freight to the agreed destination port. Shipping line and route coordinated according to customer requirements and commercial agreement.

08

Shipping Notification

Customer notified of shipment details — vessel name, container number, estimated arrival date and document dispatch confirmation.

09

Customer Delivery

Product delivered to the agreed destination. Post-delivery follow-up with the customer to confirm receipt and satisfaction with the shipment.
