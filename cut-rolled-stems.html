Why CRES Is Essential for Modern High-Speed Cigarette Manufacturing

Tobacco leaf naturally consists of approximately 75% lamina and 25% stem. In untreated form, stems are rigid, dense, and unsuitable for fine smoking articles.

At Prime Leaf Processing, our dedicated Cut Rolled Expanded Stems (CRES) line unlocks the hidden structural potential of the leaf stem. Through controlled superheated steaming, heavy-gauge rolling below 0.2mm, micro-cutting, and rapid high-temperature air expansion (250°C–300°C), stem cells are expanded by up to 150%.

The resulting fibers form elastic, curled, interlocking strands up to 50mm in length. Unlike brittle stem flakes that crumble into dust, PLP's CRES meshes perfectly with cut-rag lamina, maintaining rod firmness while dramatically reducing blend density.

Economic Impact Formula:

At a typical 20% to 30% CRES inclusion rate in standard American or European blends, cigarette manufacturers achieve an immediate 10% to 15% overall tobacco weight reduction per 1,000 sticks, significantly lowering import duty overheads and leaf consumption.

Factory Line Architecture

The 9-Stage CRES Expansion Production Flow

Operated at our BEPZA EZ Chattogram plant with fully automated PLC controls and continuous inline monitoring.

1

Stem Cleaning & Destoning

Air-density separation and mechanical screening remove sand, silica, foreign matter, and soil particles.

Silica Purity: > 99.2%
2

Pressurized Steam Conditioning

High-pressure saturated steam softens the rigid cellular matrix and elevates moisture evenly to 28%–32%.

Temp: 90°C – 100°C | Moisture: ~30%
3

Precision Heavy Rolling

Opposing heavy-duty hardened steel rollers flatten stems into flexible ribbons, rupturing dense cell walls without fiber fracture.

Roller Gap: ≤ 0.20 mm
4

Ultra-Fine Rotary Cutting

High-speed rotary knife drums slice flattened stems into micro-width strands, creating long, narrow interlocking filaments.

Cut Width: 0.10 mm – 0.20 mm
5

Flash Air Expansion (250°C–300°C)

Superheated hot air instantly vaporizes internal moisture, ballooning cell structures outward and locking in high-volume curl.

Cell Expansion: +150% Volume
6

Fluidized Bed Drying & Stabilizing

Gentle hot-air fluidized bed brings moisture down to the exact customer target while preventing strand breakage.

Target Moisture: 12.0% ± 0.5%
7

Aspiration & Dust Separation

Multi-stage cyclone separator strips away microscopic fines and broken fragments, guaranteeing pure clean fiber strands.

Fines < 1mm: < 1.5%
8

Automated Quality QA Station

Continuous inline NIR sensors measure real-time moisture, density, and Borgwaldt filling volume before packaging.

Tolerance: ±0.5% Moisture
9

C-48 Carton Packing & Palletizing

Hydraulic pressing into standard C-48 export cartons (200 kg net) lined with sealed polyethylene bags for marine transit.

Net Weight: 200 kg / C-48 Carton
